# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [中文](docs/zh-CH/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the BSWorld community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, experiences, and technical approaches;
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes, correcting them, and learning from them;
- focusing discussion on the project and the needs of the wider community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith participation;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, chat channels, events, and other spaces managed by the project. It also applies when someone officially represents BSWorld in a public space.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately by emailing [menglingao@xiaomi.com](mailto:menglingao@xiaomi.com). Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details.

Project maintainers will review reports fairly, protect the privacy and safety of reporters, and take action appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. Depending on the severity and history of the behavior, consequences may include a private correction, formal warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.

Anyone responsible for handling a report must disclose a conflict of interest and withdraw from that case. Retaliation against a person who reports an incident or participates in an investigation is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.
